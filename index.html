Framing & brand style
Motion & sound timing

Follow face tracks a prominent face and keeps the full scene when none is found. It cannot identify the speaking person in every group shot. Framing holds per scene and moves only after sustained movement. Motion is off by default; sound accents respect the Sound design setting.

Bubbles & sticker accents

Small animated accents appear on transcript cues such as questions, money, success, or emphasis. Up to roughly three per minute, with at least 12 seconds between them. They can overlap source graphics; switch them off for busy footage.
